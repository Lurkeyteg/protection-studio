Eight boxes. How this is calculated. Each box shows the requirement, what is already in place, and the shortfall. Requirements follow the LIA / MAS Basic Financial Planning Guide: 9× annual income for death and TPD (LIA's own FAQ says 9–10×) and 4× annual income for standard critical illness (his brief: 4–5×), with the multiples set in the sidebar rather than buried in code. National schemes (DPS, MediShield Life, CareShield Life) are counted as resources where they actually pay.
  1. Death, TPD and critical illness are alternative claims, not additive — if the CI cover is accelerated (pays out of the death sum assured) a CI claim reduces the death cover that remains.
  2. The accidental split funds part of the 9× with cheaper accident-only cover. On an accident the two pay together; on any other cause only the base cover pays, so the accidental share is a deliberate shortfall — see the claim-scenario strip in each box.
  3. Group cover is counted, but it ends with the job. Box 8 shows how much of the plan is contingent.
  4. Recommended additions are shown in their own colour and never mixed into "in place" — a recommendation is not protection until the client takes up the policy.
  5. Statutory figures carry an effective-date note in their tooltip; no figure is typed from memory.
